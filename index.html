The approach

You've been selected for the drop.

A week before the event, every C6 owner, plus 30 chosen press, riders and creators, receives a military-spec wooden crate, hand-stenciled with their name and a drop number.

Inside the crate

  • Vintage air-mail invitation, hand-typed
  • Embroidered Flying Flea patch (numbered)
  • Enamel parachute pin
  • Hand-folded silk square (parachute fabric)
  • Tin of pressed-butter biscuits, ration-style
  • Coordinates of the venue. Nothing else.

The Drop

Uncrate the History

Each F6 arrives the way the original Flea did in World War II, sealed inside a raw wooden crate, stamped with military stencils and wartime markings, exactly as paratroopers received them before D-Day drops. Break it open, and the inside walls reveal a limited-edition art piece commissioned exclusively for that bike, yours to frame, keep, or hang. The crate is the first chapter of your ownership story.
